# InventoryWizard Wiki

InventoryWizard is a client-side mod for Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge and Forge that sorts your inventory, hotbar and containers with one key press or click. There's also a Paper/Spigot plugin that sorts for every player on a server, with no client mod needed.

## Features
- **Smart categorization** - Items are grouped logically (weapons, tools, food, blocks, etc.) using item tags and components, so modded items are sorted too
- **Hotbar optimization** - Weapons and tools prioritized for survival gameplay
- **Stack consolidation** - Automatically combines partial stacks
- **Configurable partial stack sorting** - Choose whether leftover partial stacks are sorted in or placed at the end (see [Configuration](Configuration#partial-stack-sorting-behavior))
- **Component preservation** - Enchanted, named or otherwise different items are never merged together
- **Covers everything** - Player inventory, hotbar, chests (incl. double, trapped, chest minecarts and chest boats), shulker boxes, barrels and ender chests
